What happens after you reach out

  1. A conversation about the person. Who will use the tub or shower, how they move, and what part of bathing is hardest today.
  2. A conversation about the room. The size of the current tub space, the drain side, the doorways, and whether the bathroom is upstairs.
  3. Options. The specialist explains which tub or shower styles fit, which features make sense, and which parts of your home are likely to affect the quote.
  4. Installation or delivery. Where professional installation is available, the specialist explains how it is scheduled and who does the work. If you have your own licensed contractor, you can ask about delivery of the tub only.
  5. Your decision, in your time. Take the written information, talk it over with family, and call back when you are ready.

What to have ready

  • The ZIP code where the tub will be installed
  • Rough length and width of the current tub space (see our measuring guide)
  • Whether the drain is on the left or right
  • Photos of the tub area, the bathroom door, the water heater label and the electrical panel, if you can
  • Any mobility aids the person uses
  • Whether you plan to use a funding program, such as the VA HISA benefit, so the paperwork can be prepared first

Is this an emergency?

If someone has fallen and is hurt, cannot get up, or may have hit their head, call 911. For urgent concerns about an older adult's safety or possible abuse, contact your county's Adult Protective Services. Our team handles bathroom products and installation, not emergencies.
